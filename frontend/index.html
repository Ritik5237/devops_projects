FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? Here are quick answers to common queries about TaskFlow.

Is TaskFlow free to use?

Yes! You can create a free account immediately to organize and manage all your tasks.

Is my data secure and private?

Yes. All accounts use salted bcrypt password hashing and token-based authentication. Your tasks are isolated to your account only.

Can I use TaskFlow on mobile devices?

Absolutely. TaskFlow is fully responsive and optimized for desktops, tablets, and smartphones.

How do I get started?

Simply click "Get Started" to create your free account in seconds with your name and email.
